Before you begin

Your task

A farmer in your barangay grows tomatoes in a community garden. On a hot afternoon, the leaves stay firm and green as long as the roots take in enough water. Explain how the roots, stem, and leaves work together to move water through the plant, and why the tube-like structure of the xylem suits this job.

Write about 5 to 8 complete sentences (at least 40 words).
